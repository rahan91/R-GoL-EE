Simulation Rules

B = neighbours needed to birth a cell · S = neighbours needed to survive. Type digits 0–9 into either field.

Board Grid

Set the board size with X and Y, then leave the box. With auto-fit on the board resizes to fill the window; turn it off to keep the cell size and pan/zoom around a larger board instead.

Cell Lifecycle

Cells age through the spectrum every generation they survive — Blue→Green is quick, while the final Purple→White crawl takes 80 generations. Pure white = 180 generations old.

Addable Modules

Pick a module, then click the board to drop it. Click it again (or press Esc) to exit placement. Pan/zoom to find a good spot first.
